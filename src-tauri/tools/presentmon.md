Vortex Optimizer launches the console collector with `--output_stdout`, `--v2_metrics`, and `--exclude_dropped`. PresentMon consumes Windows ETW presentation events and emits CSV samples, which the Tauri backend parses into FPS, frame time, and p95 frame-time statistics.
